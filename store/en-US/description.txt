AFib Monitor is a heart rhythm diary that works with its own wearable ECG sensor.

WHAT IT DOES
• Live heart rate and ECG from the sensor over Bluetooth
• 10-second ECG recordings on demand or together with a symptom note
• Symptom diary: palpitations, weakness, dizziness, shortness of breath
• Poincaré plot and a signal quality index, so you can see how clean a recording is
• A report for your doctor: you enter your doctor's code, and only that doctor sees your data
• Telegram notifications for relatives — only the ones you invite
• Russian, Kyrgyz and English

YOUR DATA
• Download everything as one file or delete your account from your profile
• See which doctor opened your data and when
• We do not sell data and show no ads

IMPORTANT
AFib Monitor is not a medical device, does not diagnose and does not replace a doctor. Make treatment decisions only with your doctor. For chest pain, fainting or severe shortness of breath, call your local emergency number.

ECG recording requires the AFib Monitor sensor.
